Questions

Everything You
Want to Know.

Is Vedha a mixer grinder or a wet grinder?
+
Vedha is a new category entirely. It combines the compact form of a mixer grinder with specialized engineering to deliver wet-grinder-like batter consistency. It's neither — it's what replaces both for the modern Indian kitchen.
Will the batter actually be as soft as a wet grinder?
+
The combination of high-torque motor, precision blade geometry, and intelligent flow dynamics is specifically engineered to create the aeration and circulation that produces fluffy idli batter — a result not achievable with conventional mixer jars.
Will the batter get stuck like it does in my regular mixy?
+
No. Vedha's Intelligent Flow Dynamics system is designed specifically to prevent ingredient locking — the most common failure point of standard mixer jars during wet grinding. The jar geometry and blade system work together to keep ingredients in motion.
How much counter space does Vedha need?
+
Vedha sits within the standard mixer grinder footprint — dramatically smaller than a wet grinder. Designed specifically for compact modern kitchens and nuclear family homes.
Is it suitable for daily use by a nuclear family?
+
Absolutely — this is precisely the use case Vedha was engineered for. The motor, materials, and thermal management are all rated for regular daily use cycles.
